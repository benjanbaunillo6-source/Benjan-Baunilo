🏛️ About UC

Excellence in Education Since 1964

The University of Cebu Main Campus has been a beacon of quality and affordable education in the Visayas region. We are committed to nurturing well-rounded, competent, and values-driven graduates who make a difference in the community.

🎯

Our Mission

Provide quality, affordable education to every deserving Filipino student.

🌟

Our Vision

A globally competitive university producing leaders of character and competence.

🤝

Core Values

Integrity, Excellence, Service, and Commitment to community development.

🏅

Accreditation

CHED-recognized programs with multiple AACCUP-accredited courses.
